# Scene gallery

Screenshots of every scene, rendered headlessly by `Tools/GolfVR/Capture Scene Gallery` (`GolfVR.EditorTools.SceneGalleryScreenshots.Run`). Regenerate them after changing a scene.
The 1-5 keys switch between the scenes while the app is running.
